Science: How science works

Science uses observation, questions, testing and evidence to explain the natural world.

Open lesson

Key idea: A scientific explanation should be testable and open to revision when stronger evidence appears.

Study next: Matter, energy, cells, forces and the scientific method.

💼

Business: Creating value

A business solves a customer problem by offering a product or service.

Open lesson

Key idea: Understand customer needs, costs, pricing, competition and cash flow before scaling.

Study next: Market research, marketing, accounting and business models.

🤖

Technology: AI basics

Artificial intelligence describes computer systems performing tasks associated with human intelligence.

Open lesson

Key idea: Many AI systems learn patterns from data. Their outputs can be useful but may also be wrong or biased.

Study next: Data, algorithms, machine learning and responsible AI.

🏛️

History: Reading the past

History studies past events using evidence such as documents, objects and accounts.

Open lesson

Key idea: Compare sources, consider who created them, and distinguish evidence from interpretation.

Study next: Ancient societies, trade, empires and modern history.

🌍

Geography: Our changing planet

Geography explores places, environments and how people interact with them.

Open lesson

Key idea: Physical geography studies natural systems; human geography studies people, places and societies.

Study next: Maps, climate, population and resources.

Mathematics: Reasoning with numbers

Mathematics helps describe patterns, quantities, shapes and relationships.

Open lesson

Key idea: Show your working, check assumptions and test whether an answer makes sense.

Study next: Arithmetic, algebra, geometry, probability and statistics.

📈 A practical learning method

1. Choose one question
2. Start with the basics
3. Compare reliable sources
4. Take short notes
5. Explain it in your own words
6. Review and apply it

Understanding grows through curiosity, practice and checking evidence — not simply memorising facts.

💡 Topic of the day

How does the internet work?

The internet is a global network of connected computer networks. Devices exchange information using agreed rules called protocols. Websites are delivered to your browser by servers.
